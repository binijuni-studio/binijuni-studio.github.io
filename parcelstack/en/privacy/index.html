Privacy policy

This policy explains how BiniJuni (“we”) handles information in Parcel Stack (the “App”) and on this official website.

Effective and last updated: November 22, 2026

Game progress stays on your device. No account is required. Advertising services may process information in ad-enabled versions, the App collects usage statistics with Firebase Analytics to improve the game, and we receive the information you choose to send by email.

Information stored on your device

Completed and unlocked stages, recently played stages, in-progress parcel placements, sound and vibration settings, hint rewards, and records needed to manage ad frequency are stored on your device. We do not upload your game save file to our own server or link it to an account. The App does not provide cloud synchronization or cross-device restoration. Operating-system backup and restore behavior may depend on your device settings.

The App does not require your name, phone number, email address, or contacts to play. There is no game account to close.

Information advertising services may process

Ad-enabled versions use Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK, provided by Google LLC. When an ad is requested, the SDK may process the following depending on its version, configuration, and your consent choices:

  • Advertising identifiers and other app- or device-scoped identifiers, device and operating-system information.
  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it.
  • Ad impressions, clicks, video views, rewards, and other ad interactions.
  • Crash, diagnostic, performance, and app-interaction information.
  • Ad consent choices required for your region and applicable law.

Purposes include serving and measuring ads, personalized advertising where applicable conditions such as consent are met, improving service quality, and preventing fraud. Not every item is collected for every user. Rewarded hint ads are requested when you choose to watch one. Basic puzzle play remains available if you decline advertising consent or tracking, although hints that require a rewarded ad may be unavailable.

See the Google Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from partner apps, and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures.

App usage statistics (Firebase Analytics)

The App uses Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) by Google LLC to tune difficulty and improve the game. It does not send your name, email, or other information that directly identifies you. The following may be processed:

  • App instance ID and advertising identifier (subject to device settings and tracking permission)
  • Automatically collected usage such as installs, app opens, and session duration
  • Game progress events such as level start and completion (level number)
  • Device model, operating system, app version, language, and country/region estimated from the IP address

We use this to understand usage and where players stop, and to fix errors and difficulty. It may be combined with AdMob information to measure ad revenue. Event-level data is kept for two months under the Firebase data retention setting and then deleted. User-level data such as the app instance ID is kept for 14 months after your last use, and the period restarts if you use the App again within it. Aggregated reports are handled under Google’s policies. In regions that require consent, such as the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, your choice in the App’s consent form is applied to analytics through Google Consent Mode. See Privacy and Security in Firebase for details.

Your advertising choices

  • In regions that require consent, your choice in the App applies to both advertising and usage statistics (Firebase Analytics).
  • Use the advertising consent form when it is presented. Where required for your region, you can revisit choices using the ad privacy option shown in the App’s settings.
  • On iOS, manage tracking under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, if the App appears there.
  • On Android, use your device’s privacy or advertising settings to manage its advertising identifier. Menu names vary.
  • You can also review relevant settings in Google My Ad Center. Limiting personalized advertising does not remove all ads.

Support messages and retention

If you contact us, we receive your email address, message, and any screenshots or device details you choose to include. We use this information to investigate issues, reply, handle requests, and address related disputes. We keep it only as long as needed for these purposes, or for a legally required retention period, then delete it. We do not collect support information unless you send it.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, identification documents, advertising identifiers, or other unnecessary sensitive information. Hide other people’s personal information in screenshots.

Deletion and your rights

You can remove local game data by clearing the App’s data or deleting the App. Deleting the App also resets the app instance ID used for statistics. Because we do not link statistics to individuals, we may be unable to locate and delete individual statistics records. Manage any operating-system backups separately through your device or backup service. Deletion or reinstallation may erase progress; we cannot restore local game records from a server.

Contact the email below to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or withdrawal of consent for personal information we hold, such as support messages. We will explain the rights and identity-verification steps that apply to your request. Advertising providers also offer their own privacy tools and contact channels.

Children’s information

The App does not require registration or a date of birth. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information. If a parent or guardian believes a child has supplied personal information through a support message or otherwise, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate deletion steps.

Security and this website

We restrict access to support materials to what is needed for handling requests and do not ask for unnecessary personal information. This website has no separate analytics tool, advertising-tracking script, or registration form. Fonts and images are served from this site. Please review the policies of the hosting service and any external links you choose to visit.
