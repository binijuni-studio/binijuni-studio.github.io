# Parcel Stack website assets

- `app-icon.webp`, `apple-touch-icon.png`: size/format variants of the user-approved second app-icon design in the Parcel Stack project, `assets/app_icon/app_icon_1024.png`. The original design was generated with built-in image_gen and selected by the owner. No new artwork was generated for this website.
- `gameplay.webp`: format conversion of `build/responsive_game_440.png`, a captured development screen of Parcel Stack. Its visible caption identifies it as a development image rather than a final release screenshot.
- `Jua-Regular.ttf`: unchanged Jua Regular from the app's bundled font. Copyright 2018 The Jua Project Authors. Distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the full notice is preserved in `OFL-Jua.txt`. Official source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/jua

The site uses local assets only. No external font, analytics, advertisement, or video script is loaded. Official Google and GitHub privacy documents are ordinary links, not embedded services.
