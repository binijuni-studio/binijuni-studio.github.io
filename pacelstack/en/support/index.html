Frequently asked questions

Is my progress saved automatically?

Completed stages, in-progress placements, and settings are saved on your device. There is no registration or in-app cloud sync, and progress does not automatically transfer to another device. We cannot restore local saves from a server.

How do I move and rotate parcels?

Drag a parcel from the tray or truck to the desired position. Select a parcel and use Rotate to turn it, or Undo to reverse your previous action. Use the tray’s left and right buttons to find other parcels.

All parcels are packed. Why can’t I depart?

Check that every parcel is inside the board without overlaps. In delivery stages, parcels must also be removable in number order. If another parcel blocks an earlier delivery’s way out, try changing the arrangement.

How do hints work?

Choose a hint and finish watching its rewarded ad. A placement position and orientation will be shown for 5 seconds. Closing early or not receiving reward confirmation does not grant the hint. If no ad is ready, try again later.

An ad won’t load, or I didn’t receive a hint.

Check your network connection and ad consent choices. Ad availability can vary by region, device, and inventory. Keep the App installed and try again later. If the issue continues, email your device and app versions, approximate time, and stage number.

Where are sound, vibration, and ad privacy settings?

Open Settings from the home or game screen to change sound effects and vibration. Where required for your region, an ad privacy option appears in Settings. You can also manage tracking and advertising permissions in your device’s privacy settings.

Can I play in English?

The App uses Korean when your device’s primary language is Korean, and English for other primary languages.

How do I request deletion of personal information?

You can remove local game data by clearing app data or deleting the App, but this also removes your progress. To request deletion of support messages or other information we hold, email us. See our privacy policy for details.

What not to send

We do not need passwords, payment-card details, or identity documents. Hide personal information in screenshots. We use the information you send to handle your support request.
